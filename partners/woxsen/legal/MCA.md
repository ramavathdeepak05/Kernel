1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 Scope of Collaboration: The Parties agree to collaborate on the design, development, deployment, testing, validation, and commercialization of the Platform. The scope encompasses the end-to-end development of eight (8) Core Modules: Admissions, Academics, Examinations, Student Services, Finance, HR & Payroll, Regulatory, and Research.
1.2 Master Framework: This Agreement serves as the master framework governing all aspects of the collaboration.
1.3 Incorporation & Order of Precedence: The Parties agree to explicitly include, attach, and incorporate by reference the following documents into this Master Collaboration Agreement. In the event of any conflict, ambiguity, or inconsistency between the documents forming this collaboration, the order of precedence shall be: (i) this Master Agreement; (ii) Schedule X (Commercial Participation & IP); (iii) Schedule X-1 (Royalty Calculation Appendix); (iv) Exhibit A: Pilot Deployment Agreement; (v) Exhibit B: Non-Disclosure Agreement; (vi) Exhibit C: Project Agreement; and (vii) Exhibit D: Statement of Work (SOW) Master Deliverables Specification. In matters relating specifically to royalties and commercialization economics, Schedule X and Schedule X-1 shall prevail exclusively over any conflicting provision in this Agreement or its Exhibits.
2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 “Accepted Deliverable” means a Module or component that has been delivered by Woxsen, rigorously tested, and formally accepted in writing by QUAICU after successfully meeting all predefined acceptance criteria and performance benchmarks.
2.2 “Background IP” means any intellectual property, materials, or methodologies owned or controlled by a Party prior to the Effective Date, or developed independently of this Agreement.
2.3 “Commercialization” means any deployment, licensing, or provisioning of the Platform (or any Module thereof) to third parties as a revenue-generating product, expressly excluding any Pilot deployments.
2.4 “Foreground IP” means all intellectual property, source code, algorithms, models, and derivatives created, developed, or reduced to practice directly arising from the collaborative activities under this Agreement.
2.5 “NetRevenue” means the actual, realized gross receipts collected by QUAICU from the Commercialization of a specific Module, strictly excluding deductions as defined in Schedule X-1.
3. STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) AND EXECUTION
3.1 SOW Issuance: The development of each specific Module shall be governed by a detailed Statement of Work (SOW) Annex. Each SOW must explicitly define the scope, technical specifications, architecture diagrams (mandating QUAICU's Local-First Architecture), delivery timelines, resource allocation, and specific acceptance criteria.
3.2 Acceptance Authority & Reset: QUAICU, acting as the Architecture Authority, reserves the sole discretion, exercised in accordance with Clause 14.5 and the acceptance criteria defined in the SOW, to approve or reject any delivered Module. Prior demonstrations or validations of Woxsen's Existing Product do not constitute acceptance for re-engineered Modules.
3.3 Acceptance Criteria & Sovereignty Test: To achieve "Accepted Deliverable" status, a Module must seamlessly integrate with the core Platform and successfully pass the "Sovereignty Test" (executing core workflows such as application processing or report generation offline/air-gapped without reliance on external cloud inference).
3.4 Performance Benchmarks: All Modules must meet enterprise-grade performance thresholds, specifically: Uptime target of 99.5%, Response latency <300ms, and System error rate <1%. All performance benchmarks shall be strictly measured using exclusively QUAICU’s internal monitoring tools. Failure to meet performance benchmarks for two consecutive reporting periods shall trigger remediation obligations, including defined service credits or corrective action plans.
3.5 Code Ring-Fencing and Compartmentalized Access: Woxsen’s access to QUAICU’s Monorepo, cloud infrastructure, and databases shall be strictly compartmentalized and limited only to the specific repositories expressly required for the active SOW. Unauthorized access to non-SOW modules or the core AI orchestrator constitutes a material breach of this Agreement.
3.6 Continuity of Development: QUAICU shall have the unrestricted right to continue development, modification, and commercialization of the Platform independently or with third parties at any time, without reliance on Woxsen, and Woxsen shall have no right to object to or restrict such continuation.
4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS & ARCHITECTURE COMPLIANCE
4.1 Baseline Background IP: The Parties explicitly acknowledge and agree that QUAICU has independently engineered seventy percent (70%) of the Platform prior to this Agreement. This pre-existing 70% baseline codebase, architecture, and infrastructure remains the absolute and exclusive Background IP of QUAICU. Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in their respective Background IP.
4.1(a) Background IP License Grant: In the event Woxsen incorporates any of its Background IP into an Accepted Deliverable, Woxsen hereby grants QUAICU a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable, and sublicensable license to use, modify, and commercialize such Woxsen Background IP as integrated into the Platform.
4.2 Module-Level IP, Joint Ownership & Resale Restrictions: Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, for any Foreground IP explicitly co-developed by the Parties to complete the remaining thirty percent (30%) of a Module, such newly created Foreground IP shall be jointly shared and owned by QUAICU and Woxsen. However, to ensure unified go-to-market execution, Woxsen hereby grants QUAICU the sole, perpetual, irrevocable, exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable, and sublicensable right and license to use, modify, rewrite, and commercialize such jointly-owned Foreground IP.
Strict Restriction on Woxsen: Despite Woxsen's joint ownership of the Foreground IP delta, Woxsen shall have no right to modify, resell, sublicense, distribute, or otherwise commercialize any co-developed Module, the Platform, or the underlying architecture to any third party without the express, prior written consent of QUAICU. QUAICU retains the sole and exclusive right to direct all commercialization efforts.
For avoidance of doubt, joint ownership shall not grant Woxsen any independent right to use, exploit, license, or commercialize such Foreground IP except as expressly permitted under this Agreement.
4.3 Rewrite and Versioning Rights: QUAICU retains the sole discretion, exercised in accordance with Clause 14.5, to rewrite, refactor, replace, or discontinue any Module (or any part thereof) at any time. Should QUAICU rewrite a Module, Woxsen’s royalty entitlements shall terminate with respect to such subsequent version; provided, however, that if a rewritten version retains substantially similar functionality, workflows, or architecture derived from the Accepted Deliverable, Woxsen shall continue to receive a reduced royalty equal to a minimum of thirty percent (30%) of its prior applicable Royalty Rate.
Substantially similar shall require demonstrable reuse of material portions of code, architecture, or proprietary logic from the Accepted Deliverable, and shall not include similarity in high-level functionality, business outcomes, or user workflows.
4.4 Permitted Cloud Search Exception: Transmitting sensitive, personal, or institutional data to external cloud APIs is prohibited. The sole exception is for non-determinative search or indexing functions, provided such integrations transmit only sanitized/anonymized metadata and do not impair core functionality if the cloud service fails.
4.5 Mandatory Downstream IP Agreements: Prior to permitting any student, researcher, faculty member, or contractor to contribute to the Platform, Woxsen represents and warrants that it shall execute legally binding intellectual property assignment agreements with such individuals, irrevocably assigning all Foreground IP rights to Woxsen to ensure compliance with Clause 4.2.
4.6 Enterprise Valuation and M&A Waiver: Woxsen acknowledges that its economic entitlements under this Agreement are strictly limited to the royalties generated from commercial software sales as defined in Clause 5. Woxsen hereby expressly waives any and all claims to QUAICU’s enterprise valuation, equity, or any proceeds derived from the sale, merger, acquisition, or liquidation of QUAICU as a corporate entity.
4.7 Absolute Prohibition on Copyleft Code: Woxsen strictly represents and warrants that no code, library, or dependency governed by a Copyleft, Viral, or reciprocal open-source license (including but not limited to GPL, AGPL, or CC-BY-SA) shall be introduced into the Platform. Any such introduction constitutes a material, incurable breach, and Woxsen shall bear the full cost of rewriting the contaminated code and any resulting legal liabilities.
4.8 Platform Indivisibility: The Platform shall be treated as a unified system and not as separable modules for ownership or control purposes. Any jointly developed components shall not grant Woxsen any rights over the Platform as a whole.
5. ROYALTY AND CONTRIBUTION FRAMEWORK
5.1 Baseline Contribution Acknowledgment: The Parties formally recognize that QUAICU’s pre-existing Background IP constitutes a permanent, baseline contribution of seventy percent (70%) to every Module.
5.2 Contribution Scoring Rubric: For the remaining 30% of development, Woxsen's Contribution Percentage shall be evaluated on a 100-point scale.
5.3 Royalty Formula: Woxsen's raw rubric score shall be mathematically capped to reflect the 30% development delta. The Royalty payable to Woxsen for Accepted Deliverables shall be calculated strictly using the following formula:
Royalty = (Woxsen Rubric Score / 100) × 0.25 × 0.35 × NetRevenue.
5.4 NetRevenue Definition & Exclusions: "NetRevenue" strictly means the actual gross receipts collected, less the exclusions detailed and defined within Schedule X-1 (Section 2).
5.5 Reporting & Payment: QUAICU shall provide Woxsen with a quarterly Royalty Statement detailing NetRevenue calculations within thirty (30) days of the calendar quarter's end. Payment of undisputed amounts shall be made within forty-five (45) days.
5.6 Unauthorized Resale Disgorgement: Notwithstanding QUAICU's exclusive commercialization rights, in the event that Woxsen commercializes, resells, or provisions any co-developed Module to its own clients, affiliates, or any third party in breach of this Agreement, Woxsen shall be strictly liable to pay QUAICU liquidated damages equal to the actual loss suffered by QUAICU or one hundred percent (100%) of all gross revenues generated from such transactions, whichever is lower. Furthermore, QUAICU shall be entitled to immediate injunctive relief.
5.7 QUAICU Offensive Audit Right: QUAICU shall have the right, upon fifteen (15) days written notice, to audit Woxsen’s deployment logs, IT infrastructure, and relevant commercial records strictly to verify compliance with the resale restrictions (Clause 4.2), the non-compete (Clause 7.4), and to ensure no unauthorized instances of the Platform have been provisioned. If unauthorized commercialization or deployment is discovered, Woxsen shall bear the full cost of the audit in addition to the liabilities under Clause 5.6.
6. PILOT FRAMEWORK (STRICTLY DEFINED)
6.1 Immediate Pilot Launch at Woxsen:QUAICU shall have the sole and absolute right to launch, deploy, and operate the Pilot within Woxsen University’s institutional environment as a designated Pilot site.
Woxsen shall provide all necessary institutional access, infrastructure support, data access (as permitted by law), and operational cooperation required to enable such Pilot deployments.
Such deployment shall not require any additional approval or consent from Woxsen, and coordination shall be limited strictly to logistical and implementation purposes.
Woxsen shall not unreasonably delay, restrict, or condition such Pilot deployment.
6.2 Pilot Scope & Limitations: A Pilot shall be strictly scoped to a maximum of 3,000 active users, across required institutional departments, for a defined duration of 10 to 18 months. Deployments are characterized as "Proof-of-Concept" irrespective of scale.
6.3 Commercial Handling of Pilots: QUAICU reserves the right to charge Pilot Clients administrative fees, setup costs, hosting fees, or facilitation charges. Any amounts charged compensate QUAICU for Pilot-related Services and do not constitute consideration for the Pilot Product itself. Total Pilot-related fees shall not exceed a commercially reasonable threshold relative to equivalent early-stage enterprise pilot programs, and shall not constitute full commercial licensing fees.
6.4 Royalty Exclusion: Under no circumstances shall Woxsen be entitled to any royalty, revenue share, or financial compensation derived from Pilot deployments.
6.5 Expiration of Pilot: Upon the expiration of the Pilot duration, the deployment shall not automatically convert to a Commercialization phase. Any transition from a Pilot to a royalty-bearing Commercialization phase requires a separate, mutually executed, written addendum.
7. CLIENT OWNERSHIP AND NON-CIRCUMVENTION
7.1 Exclusive Client Ownership: All institutional clients, pilot partners, and prospective leads introduced, engaged, or managed by QUAICU during the term of this Agreement shall remain the exclusive clients of QUAICU.
7.2 Non-Solicitation & Non-Circumvention (Clients): Woxsen, its affiliates, employees, and representatives are strictly restricted from directly or indirectly soliciting, engaging, contracting with, or attempting to circumvent QUAICU to provide similar software products or services to any QUAICU client or prospective client introduced during a Pilot or Commercialization phase. This restriction applies during the Term and for twenty-four (24) months following termination.
7.3 Non-Solicitation of Personnel: During the Term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following its expiration or termination, Woxsen shall not directly or indirectly solicit, induce, recruit, or encourage any employee, contractor, or consultant of QUAICU to leave their employment or engagement with QUAICU.
7.4 Strict Non-Compete:Woxsen shall not use any confidential Information, proprietary architecture, or technical know-how obtained under this Agreement to develop or enable any competing platform, whether during the Term or thereafter. 
8. GOVERNANCE AND PROJECT MANAGEMENT
8.1 Steering Committee: The Parties shall establish a joint steering committee comprising authorized representatives from both sides. Any technical, architectural, or product roadmap disagreements shall be escalated to the steering committee. In the event of a deadlock, QUAICU, acting as the primary investor and commercializing entity, shall have the final, tie-breaking decision-making authority. 10.2 Obligations: The Receiving Party shall hold all confidential information in strict confidence, use it solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
11. DATA PROTECTION, AI TRAINING, AND SECURITY

QUAICU’s right to implement a remote "kill switch" to protect its trade secrets may be exercised only in the case of a material breach, with prior written notice where feasible, and shall not disrupt critical institutional operations without emergency justification.

SCHEDULE X
COMMERCIAL PARTICIPATION, ROYALTIES & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
1. Definitions
1.1 "Platform" means the software platform, system or product being collaboratively developed under the Agreement, including all Modules, components, services, source code, object code, documentation, configurations, AI pipelines, data models and any derivatives.
1.2 "Module" means an independently deployable functional unit of the Platform, as classified by QUAICU.
1.3 "Accepted Deliverable" means a Module (or component) that has been delivered by Woxsen and formally accepted in writing by QUAICU pursuant to the agreed deliverables checklist.
1.4 "Royalty" means the revenue share payable to Woxsen under this Schedule, calculated on a per-Module basis as set forth herein.
1.5 "Version" means any revision, release, build, fork, rewrite, refactor, enhancement, replacement or modification of a Module.
2. Collaboration & Strategic Participation
2.1 QUAICU acknowledges Woxsen's strategic partnership role and the value of Woxsen's contributions to the Platform. QUAICU agrees to treat Woxsen as a non-exclusive strategic collaborator, without any obligation on QUAICU to provide future work or participation for any commercialization activities directly related to Modules materially developed by Woxsen under this Agreement.
2.2 To give effect to that partnership role, QUAICU will, prior to commercialization of any Module materially developed by Woxsen and accepted by QUAICU:
(a) provide written notice to Woxsen of QUAICU's intent to commercialize that Module (the "Commercialization Notice"); and
(b) permit Woxsen a period of thirty (30) calendar days from receipt of the Commercialization Notice to submit a written proposal for commercial participation or other commercial terms (the "Participation Proposal").
2.3 Notwithstanding clause 2.2, QUAICU retains final decision-making authority over commercialization terms and whether to accept Woxsen's Participation Proposal. QUAICU may review Woxsen’s Participation Proposal at its discretion; provided however that QUAICU shall retain sole and absolute discretion to accept, reject, or modify such proposal without obligation to provide detailed justification, subject only to Clause 14.7 of the Master Agreement.
3. Module Commercialization Model
3.1 The Platform shall be commercialized on a Module-by-Module basis. Each Module shall be a distinct commercial unit for pricing, distribution and Royalty purposes.
3.2 QUAICU retains sole authority to:
(a) determine whether any Module will be commercialized;
(b) set the commercial price and commercial packaging for any Module; and
(c) determine channels, discounts and enterprise pricing.
3.3 QUAICU's exercise of authority under clause 3.2 is subject to the notice and consultation obligations in Section 2 in respect of Modules materially developed by Woxsen.
4. Royalty Eligibility & Scope
4.1 Woxsen shall be eligible to receive Royalties only for Modules that:
(a) were materially developed by Woxsen under this Agreement; and
(b) are Accepted Deliverables.
4.2 Royalties shall not apply to:
(a) advisory or conceptual input not captured in Accepted Deliverables;
(b) student projects, prototypes or research outputs not accepted by QUAICU;
(c) Platform-level licensing, integrations, or custom deployments unless expressly agreed in writing; or
(d) Versions of a Module developed by QUAICU after the Acceptance Date in accordance with Section 5.
5. Royalty Allocation & Determination
5.1 For the avoidance of doubt, no Module shall be deemed ‘solely developed by Woxsen’ where such Module relies on, integrates with, or is built upon QUAICU Background IP, architecture, infrastructure, or platform components. All Modules under this Agreement shall be deemed jointly developed unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing by QUAICU.
5.2 Jointly developed Modules. If a Module is jointly developed by QUAICU and Woxsen (including work by students engaged by QUAICU):
(a) Woxsen's Royalty share shall be mathematically capped to reflect the thirty percent (30%) development delta as detailed in Schedule X-1, resulting in a maximum effective Royalty rate of eight point seven-five percent (8.75%) of NetRevenue;
(b) QUAICU will determine a specific Royalty percentage for each Module based on documented contribution factors (engineering effort, ownership of components, scope of responsibility and delivery risk), and shall provide Woxsen with a written explanation of the determination at or prior to commercialization; and
(c) QUAICU shall retain no less than thirty percent (30%) of the Royalty value for each jointly developed Module.
5.3 QUAICU's Royalty determinations are final and binding except in cases of demonstrable mathematical error or fraud.
6. Versioning, Rewrites and Termination of Royalty Rights
6.1 Royalties payable to Woxsen apply only to the specific Version of a Module accepted by QUAICU under this Agreement (the "Accepted Version").
6.2 If QUAICU develops a subsequent Version of the Accepted Version either (i) independently, or (ii) with a third party other than Woxsen, Woxsen shall have no claim to ownership, IP rights, or Royalty entitlement in respect of such subsequent Version, provided that if a rewritten version retains substantially similar functionality, workflows, or architecture derived from the Accepted Version, Woxsen shall continue to receive a reduced royalty equal to a minimum of thirty percent (30%) of its prior applicable Royalty Rate, and provided further that QUAICU notifies Woxsen of such subsequent commercialization in accordance with Section 2 and, if applicable, provides an opportunity to submit a Participation Proposal.
6.3 Woxsen expressly waives any right to assert derivative, continuity or "based-on" claims against QUAICU arising from QUAICU's independent development activities or from changes to the Module which QUAICU elects to pursue.
7. Intellectual Property Ownership; Assignment
7.1 Ownership Structure
(a) All Background IP shall remain the sole property of the respective Party;
(b) All Foreground IP developed solely by QUAICU shall remain exclusively owned by QUAICU; and
(c) All Foreground IP co-developed by the Parties shall be jointly owned by QUAICU and Woxsen, subject to the exclusive commercialization, licensing, and exploitation rights granted to QUAICU under Clause 4.2 of the Master Agreement.
7.2 Royalty rights granted to Woxsen shall not confer any independent commercialization, licensing, governance, or veto rights, except for the limited joint ownership rights expressly defined under Clause 4.2 of the Master Agreement.
7.3 To the extent any rights in jointly developed Foreground IP vest solely in Woxsen by operation of law, Woxsen hereby assigns such rights to the Parties jointly, in accordance with Clause 4.2 of the Master Agreement.
8. Credit, Marketing & Strategic Participation
8.1 QUAICU agrees to acknowledge Woxsen as a strategic development partner in marketing materials and case studies where (and only where) Woxsen has materially contributed to the Module being referenced; such acknowledgment shall be mutually agreed prior to publication.
8.2 Where Woxsen elects to participate in joint go-to-market activities for a Module (e.g., joint demos, academic validation), QUAICU will reasonably cooperate and provide access to non-sensitive information necessary for such activities, subject to confidentiality obligations.
9. Accounting, Reporting & Payment
9.1 QUAICU will maintain commercial and accounting records in respect of commercialization of Modules.
9.2 Royalties, if any, shall be calculated and paid pursuant to a separate payment schedule to be agreed in writing by the Parties. QUAICU's accounting records shall be presumptively accurate and binding absent manifest error, fraud, or material omission; Woxsen shall have no audit or review rights except as expressly set out in Schedule X-1.
10. Non-Assertion & No Retroactive Claims
10.1 Woxsen shall not assert any claim of ownership, authorship, or entitlement against QUAICU or the Platform based on prior internal work, parallel projects, or similar products developed outside this Agreement.
10.2 Only Accepted Deliverables may be considered for Royalty eligibility under this Schedule.
11. Dispute Resolution; Good Faith
11.1 The Parties shall attempt in accordance with this Agreement to resolve any dispute concerning Royalty allocation or interpretation of this Schedule via escalation between senior executives.
11.2 Any dispute unresolved within thirty (30) calendar days of escalation shall proceed in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions in the Agreement.

Delivery of any Module by Woxsen and acceptance by QUAICU shall constitute Woxsen's acknowledgement and acceptance of the terms of this Schedule.

NOTE: This Appendix is an operational guidance document. QUAICU retains the contractual rights reserved in Schedule X (including final Royalty percentage determinations). This Appendix defines agreed calculation methods and supporting processes intended to be used in accordance with this Agreement by the Parties.
1. Core formulas - definitions & notation
Let:
NetRevenue (Module, Period) = Gross receipts actually collected by QUAICU for sales of the Module in the Period, less the deductions itemized in Section 2.
Royalty Rate(Module) = The effective percentage share for Woxsen for that Module for the Accepted Version, capped by the 30% development delta (e.g., maximum 8.75% = 0.0875).
Royalty Due(Module, Period) = NetRevenue (Module, Period) × Royalty Rate (Module)
BundleAllocationFactor (Module) = When a sale is a bundle including multiple Modules, the fraction allocated to a Module based on Standalone Value (see Section 4).
Currency = Official currency agreed (e.g., INR / USD). All calculations use Currency after conversion of foreign receipts at QUAICU's daily bank rate; QUAICU's ledger is definitive.
Basic Royalty equation (single-module sale):
Royalty Due = NetRevenue × Royalty Rate
Bundled sale (multi-module):
For a bundled sale with Gross Bundle Revenue, allocate to Module i as:
AllocatedRevenue_i = GrossBundleRevenue × BundleAllocationFactor(Module_i)
then apply RoyaltyDue_i = (AllocatedRevenue_i - Deductions_i) × RoyaltyRate(Module_i)
2. What is deductible from Gross Receipts to compute NetRevenue
From gross invoiced revenue attributable to a Module, QUAICU will deduct:
Customer refunds and fully documented chargebacks related to that sale;
Sales taxes, VAT, GST, and similar pass-through taxes actually remitted to authorities;
Payment processor fees and currency conversion fees directly attributable to the transaction (documented);
Third-party reseller or channel commissions (documented contractual commission);
Reasonable, documented returns / credits;
Cloud hosting, API, and infrastructure costs directly attributable to the deployment; and
Direct marketing, advertising, and promotional expenses.
All deductions must be (i) incurred via third parties, (ii) directly attributable to the commercialization of the specific Module, and (iii) supported by documentary evidence. Aggregate deductions applied against Gross Receipts to calculate NetRevenue shall not exceed forty percent (40%) of such gross receipts in any given Period, unless mutually agreed otherwise in writing.
Deductions do not include QUAICU internal overhead, amortization, or R&D.
3. Timing, reporting & payment mechanics
Reporting period: Quarterly (calendar quarters) unless parties agree otherwise in writing.
Royalty statement: Within 30 days after period end QUAICU will deliver a Royalty Statement to Woxsen that includes:
Gross sales attributable to each Module (by customer and SKU);
Itemized deductions (refunds, taxes, fees, commissions, infra, marketing);
NetRevenue per Module;
Applied RoyaltyRate and computed RoyaltyDue;
Foreign exchange rates used (where applicable).
Payment due: QUAICU will pay undisputed Royalty Due within forty-five (45) days after delivery of the Royalty Statement.
Late payment: Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month (or the maximum lawful rate) from due date until paid.
Disputes: If Woxsen disputes any line item, the Parties will follow the dispute resolution process in the Agreement; undisputed portions must be paid on schedule.
4. Bundles, discounts, and allocation rules
When a sale includes multiple Modules or is sold as part of a bundle/enterprise package:
Standalone Price Allocation (preferred): Use the stand-alone price of each Module at the time of sale (QUAICU's list price) to compute allocation fractions:
BundleAllocationFactor(Module_i) = Standalone Price (Module_i) / Σ StandalonePrice(all Modules in bundle)
If Standalone prices are absent: Use Relative Cost of Development weighting (based on documented, auditable development hour rate) or mutually agreed proxies.
Discounts: Applied proportionally to allocated shares. Example:
Gross bundle price = ₹100,000, standalone prices total = ₹120,000.
Module A standalone = ₹30,000 → allocation factor = 30k/120k = 0.25 allocated revenue = 100k × 0.25 = 25k
Enterprise / custom pricing: QUAICU will propose allocation; Woxsen may request supporting rationale. QUAICU's allocation is binding unless disputed under the Agreement.
5. One-time fees, recurring subscriptions & mixed monetization
One-time license fees: Treated identically to NetRevenue.
Subscription revenue / SaaS: Recognize revenue on a cash collected basis for Royalty calculations (unless Parties mutually elect accrual basis in writing). Royalty applies to collections in the reporting Period.
Usage or meter-based charges: Calculated pro rata to usage in the Period after deductions.
6. Royalty adjustments & clawbacks
Chargebacks/refunds: If a sale previously counted in NetRevenue is refunded or charged back within 12 months and that reduces realizable revenue, QUAICU shall adjust NetRevenue in the subsequent Royalty Statement and recover corresponding overpayment from future royalties; if insufficient, Woxsen will refund the shortfall within 30 days of notification.
Compliance clawback: If an Accepted Deliverable is later found materially non-compliant with the Deliverables Checklist, QUAICU may withhold up to an agreed remediation reserve (documented) pending correction; reserve mechanics must be notified and mutually agreed prior to withholding.
7. Currency, taxes & withholding
Currency: Royalty payments are made in Currency unless otherwise agreed.
Withholding taxes: QUAICU will withhold applicable local taxes as required by law; Woxsen will provide tax documentation required to mitigate withholding.
Gross-up: No gross-up unless expressly agreed.
8. Audit & verification rights
Annual audit: Woxsen may request one limited audit of QUAICU's books relating to Royalty calculations once per 12-month period, at Woxsen's cost, by a mutually agreed independent auditor (confidentiality bound). If audit reveals an underpayment > 5% for the audited Period, QUAICU will reimburse Woxsen's audit costs and pay the shortfall with interest.
Scope: Audit limited to transactions and documentation directly relevant to Module NetRevenue for the audited Period. The audit shall not include access to pricing strategy, customer contracts, proprietary algorithms, or any information not directly required to verify NetRevenue calculations.
Access & notice: Woxsen must provide 30 days' prior notice; audits occur during normal business hours and with minimal operational disruption.
9. Applying the rubric
QUAICU retains final discretion, but Parties agree to use the following scoring rubric to evidence contribution when negotiating a RoyaltyRate for jointly developed Modules. Scores are evidentiary and feed into proposed Royalty allocation; QUAICU will consider the rubric in accordance with this Agreement when setting the final rate.
Scoring categories (total 100 points):
Core functionality ownership (30 pts): Wrote primary code implementing the Module's core business logic / algorithms. (0-30)
UI/UX ownership (15 pts): Designed and implemented primary UI flows. (0-15)
Integration & APIs (15 pts): Delivered major APIs, integration adapters, or platform connectors. (0-15)
Data & schema (10 pts): Designed DB schema, migrations, and data contracts. (0-10)
Testing & quality (10 pts): Supplied unit/integration/e2e tests and performance baselines. (0-10)
Operational artifacts (10 pts): Delivered Helm charts, runbooks, backups, and observability hooks. (0-10)
Project management & documentation (10 pts): Release notes, docs, and on-time delivery. (0-10)
Calculation:
Each Party documents contributions with evidence (PRs, commit ranges, deliverable manifests).
Scores assigned per category by QUAICU, provided with rationale.
Total score (0-100) yields a Contribution Index. This Index is applied to the 30% development delta to determine the effective Royalty Rate:
ContributionIndex (CI) = TotalScore / 100
Proposed RoyaltyRate = CI × 0.25 × 0.35
Example: CI = 0.60 (60/100 points) → Proposed RoyaltyRate = 0.60 × 0.0875 = 0.0525 (5.25% of NetRevenue).
Negotiation: The ProposedRoyaltyRate is subject to final determination by QUAICU (per Schedule X); the rubric supports transparency and documentation of contribution for negotiation and audit.
10. Worked numerical examples
All examples in Currency = INR.

Gross sales in Q1 for Module M = 2,000,000

Example B: Joint Module, bundle allocation
Bundle sale: Customer pays ₹500,000 for Bundle containing Module A and Module B.
Standalone prices: Module A = ₹150,000, Module B = ₹350,000 → total = ₹500,000.
Allocation factor: Module A = 150k/500k = 0.30; Module B = 350k/500k = 0.70.
No deductions except 2% payment fee and GST 18% (documented at invoice level; GST is passed through).
Gross bundle price = ₹500,000; GST = 90,000; payment fees = 10,000 → Net collected = 500k - 90k - 10k = 400k
AllocatedNet (Module A) = 400k × 0.30 = 120k
AllocatedNet (Module B) = 400k × 0.70 = 280k
Suppose Module A RoyaltyRate = 5.25% (0.0525), Module B Royalty Rate = 3.5% (0.035)
RoyaltyDue A = 120k × 0.0525 = ₹6,300
RoyaltyDue B = 280k × 0.035 = ₹9,800
