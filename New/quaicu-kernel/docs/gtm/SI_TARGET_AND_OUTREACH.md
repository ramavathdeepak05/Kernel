## 1. Ideal partner profile (firmographics — not named firms)

**Segments (priority order):**
1. **Big-4 / global consultancy FS-risk & AI-assurance practices** — the teams doing RBI FREE-AI / DPDP
   readiness, model-risk, and AI-governance audits for Indian banks & NBFCs. (Highest leverage: they
   define "good evidence" and carry a large regulated book.)
2. **Regional Indian SIs / consultancies** delivering tech + compliance to NBFCs, digital lenders, and
   banks — the ones the mid-market ICP actually buys through.
3. **RegTech & audit boutiques** specialised in FS compliance / model risk / internal audit.
4. **(Lower priority for a first partner)** global tech SIs without an FS-risk practice — revisit once a
   reference exists.

**Firmographic filters:**
- **Has a regulated-FS practice** — actively advises/audits banks/NBFCs on RBI/DPDP/model-risk/AI.
- **An alliance / partnerships motion** — they resell/co-sell/embed third-party platforms (not pure
  staff-aug).
- **India FS footprint** (the beachhead) — clients in the RBI-regulated mid-market.
- **AI-governance demand** — clients asking them how to *evidence* AI decisions to RBI now.

**Who to engage inside the firm:** the **alliances/partnerships lead** (commercial), the **FS-risk / AI-
assurance practice partner** (technical credibility + the audit methodology), and a **delivery lead** for
managed/embed.

## 2. Qualifying questions

- "Do you **advise or audit** financial-services clients on AI / model-risk / RBI FREE-AI / DPDP today?"
  (Confirms the practice.)
- "When a client needs to **prove** to RBI what their AI did and who approved it, what evidence do you
  rely on now?" (Surfaces the gap the Auditor View fills.)
- "Do you have an **alliances/co-sell motion** and a partner who can own it?" (Confirms a channel, not
  just delivery.)
- "Would an **independently verifiable proof bundle** your assurance team can check offline strengthen
  your AI-audit methodology?" (The wedge.)
- "Is there a **live FS account** where we could co-sell / run a joint design-partner pilot?" (Pipeline.)

## 3. Co-sell pitch narrative (compliance infrastructure)

**One-liner:** *"Be the firm whose AI audits come with cryptographic proof — and own the evidence layer."*

1. **The pressure:** RBI FREE-AI (model-risk + ex-post accountability) and DPDP push your FS clients to
   **govern and *evidence*** AI decisions — and that lands on *you* to assure.
2. **The gap:** today that evidence is logs + screenshots + the vendor's word — not something you can hand
   a regulator and stand behind.
3. **QUAICU:** a fail-closed control plane that enforces policy on AI **actions** and seals each to an
   **offline-verifiable RFC-6962 ledger** — neutral across clouds/models, sovereign (client-held keys).
   Your assurance team verifies the proof **independently, offline** (the [`AUDITOR_VIEW.md`](AUDITOR_VIEW.md)).
4. **The offer:** a **free Auditor View** for your practice + co-sell economics. When you advise/audit a
   bank's AI, QUAICU is the evidence layer you recommend — and you co-sell it into the account.
5. **The timing:** RBI FREE-AI + DPDP are landing now; the firm that standardizes the evidence format
   wins the methodology.

**Lead with:** the offline-verifiable proof bundle (run the demo), neutrality, and the
compliance-infrastructure position. **Don't oversell:** QUAICU evidences + enforces; it doesn't make the
audit compliant, and SOC 2 / pen-test / DPA are in progress (Waves 2–3).
