> standardize on QUAICU → we become compliance infrastructure**. Companion docs:

> an SI is **human**; this is the kit that lets the team land one.

## Why an SI channel (and why now)

- **The buyer's review already involves SIs.** India FS firms lean on Big-4 / consultancies for RBI
  FREE-AI + DPDP readiness, model-risk, and audit. The firm that *audits* a bank's AI is the firm that
  decides what "good evidence" looks like — so being the **evidence layer the auditor trusts** turns each
  audit into distribution.
- **Proof is auditor-native.** QUAICU's moat — an **offline-verifiable RFC-6962 proof bundle** ("what the
  AI did, under which policy, approved by whom," checkable without trusting the vendor) — is exactly what
  an auditor wants. That's the free **Auditor View** (built today — see [`AUDITOR_VIEW.md`](AUDITOR_VIEW.md)).
- **Mid-market can't DIY.** The ICP (NBFCs/digital-lenders/insurers) has no platform team; SIs are how
  they buy and deploy. An SI that standardizes on QUAICU brings a book of regulated-FS accounts.

## The wedge → the flywheel

1. Give the SI's risk/assurance practice a **free Auditor View** + enablement.
2. They use it to **evidence a client's AI governance** in an audit/advisory engagement.
3. They **recommend / mandate** QUAICU as the control + evidence layer for the client's AI.
4. The client adopts (consultation → MSA); the SI co-sells/delivers and **references the engagement**.
5. Repeat across the SI's FS book → QUAICU becomes the assumed compliance infrastructure.

## What we ask in return

- **Recommend / standardize** QUAICU as the control + evidence layer in their regulated-AI engagements
  (readiness, model-risk, audit) — when the client needs to *prove* AI governance, QUAICU is the default.
- **Co-sell** into one or more FS accounts (jointly, with deal registration).
- A **named alliance owner** on their side + a working enablement relationship.
- On a delivered engagement: a **reference** (named or anonymised) — ideally that their assurance team
  inspected the proof and it met the regulator's bar.

## Relation to the direct motion
The direct ICP motion is **consultation-led** (₹50k consultation → integrations team → MSA/order form;
`DESIGN_PARTNER_PROGRAM.md`, `docs/legal/MSA_STARTER.md`). SI deals slot in as **referral → co-sell →
managed**, with deal registration so the SI's economics are protected; an SI-sourced account still
converts on the standard quote-based MSA. Target ACV per the strategy: **$75k–$150k+**.

## Definition of success (W7-5)
- An **SI-sourced design-partner pilot**, **or** an SI that **standardizes on / mandates QUAICU's proof
  format** in a regulated-AI audit — i.e. QUAICU becomes part of how that firm evidences AI governance.
- Bonus: a co-sell pipeline of FS accounts through the SI.

## Honest notes (don't oversell)
- QUAICU is the **enforcement + evidence** layer; it does not by itself make a client (or the SI's audit)
  compliant — it produces verifiable proof the auditor evaluates. (Mirrors the not-legal-advice /
  governance-≠-compliance disclaimer in the other kits.)
- The **assurance gap** (SOC 2 / pen-test / counsel-signed DPA — Waves 2–3) is in progress; SIs doing
  vendor due-diligence will ask — have [`SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md`](../compliance/SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md) +
  [`../compliance/CAIQ_SIG_ANSWERS.md`](../compliance/CAIQ_SIG_ANSWERS.md) ready.
- The Auditor View today is the **export + offline verifier** (built); a dedicated read-only auditor
  console role is a follow-up if an SI pulls it (see [`AUDITOR_VIEW.md`](AUDITOR_VIEW.md)).
