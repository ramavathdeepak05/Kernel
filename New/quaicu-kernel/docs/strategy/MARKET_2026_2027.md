# QUAICU — Market analysis & 2026–2027 build strategy

> **Status: approved 2026-06-25.** Research basis: live web search (Jun 2026) cross-checked with an
> independent Gemini 3.1-Pro analysis. Companion docs: this repo's `ACTION_TRACKER.md` (execution),
> `PERSONAS.md` (buyer/user personas — to add), `MARKET_FIT.md` (PMF flags — to add).
> **Category we intend to own: "AI Action Governance."**

## 1. Market — real, growing, pivoting to runtime
- AI governance is a Gartner-named category set to **surpass $1B by 2030**; forecasts range ~$300–750M
  (2025/26) → $1–7B (2030–2034) at **~35–50% CAGR**. Broader AI-TRiSM/AI-security is larger (~$6B+ by
  2027). Gartner: orgs on **dedicated** AI-governance platforms are **3.4× more effective** than those
  on generic GRC.
- **The structural shift:** spend is moving from pre-deployment *model-risk + documentation* to
  **runtime / agentic governance** — enforcing on AI *actions*. The move from advisory chatbots to
  **read-write agents** (MCP standardized in 2025) flips the existential risk from *hallucination* →
  *unauthorized execution*.

## 2. Competitive map & the white space
- **Documentation/GRC** (Credo AI, Holistic AI, OneTrust, Fairly AI): registries + model risk; **blind
  at runtime**.
- **Observability** (Fiddler, Arize, Arthur, Datadog): monitoring; **not a fail-closed gate**.
- **AI/prompt firewalls** (Lakera→Check Point, Protect AI→Palo Alto, CalypsoAI, Prompt Security): secure
  the *prompt*; **not action governance or verifiable audit**. (2025–26 saw a heavy **M&A wave** here —
  hyperscalers/security incumbents buying the layer; the category is real and consolidating.)
- **Hyperscaler guardrails** (Bedrock Guardrails, Azure AI Content Safety, watsonx.governance): strong
  but **vendor-locked**.
- **WHITE SPACE / our moat — non-repudiation for AI *actions*:** fail-closed evaluate→gate→HITL→execute→
  **seal to an offline-verifiable RFC-6962 ledger**, **neutral across clouds/models**. Nobody owns
  "mathematical proof of what the AI did, under which policy, approved by whom."
- **Threats:** agent frameworks adding native HITL (integrate as the drop-in commit point, not
  friction); hyperscalers adding crypto-logging (counter with neutrality + customer-held-KMS sovereignty).

## 3. Regulatory tailwinds (accurate Jun-2026 timeline)
- **EU AI Act:** GPAI obligations live since Aug 2025; transparency Aug 2026; but the **May 2026 Digital
  Omnibus DEFERRED high-risk (Annex III) → Dec 2027** (Annex I → Aug 2028). The high-risk *cliff* (fines
  to €35M/7%, mandated lifetime event logging) is **~Dec 2027** → EU is the **2027** wave.
- **India (the better 2026 beachhead):** **RBI FREE-AI Committee report (Aug 2025), 26 recommendations**
  (model-risk mgmt, ex-post accountability, "innovation over restraint"); **DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025,
  full applicability ~May 2027**; RBI data-localization + IT-outsourcing controls make SaaS-AI without a
  dedicated/customer-KMS, every-decision-logged control plane a non-starter. The kernel already ships the
  RBI/SEBI + DPDP packs → early and aligned.

## 4. Agents / MCP / identity catalyst
- **MCP = "the AI security issue of 2026"**; **non-human-identity (NHI) governance** is the year's top
  identity-security priority (JIT, least-privilege, lifecycle). Validates **per-agent identity + governed
  tool-call interception** (the MCP-governance surface) as a sharp wedge.

## 5. Verdict — category, moat, ICP
- **Category:** **"AI Action Governance" / a cryptographic control plane** — *"Don't just observe your
  AI. Govern it — and prove it."* Sell **proof + enforcement**, not dashboards/"trust".
- **Moat:** offline-verifiable non-repudiation (Merkle + HSM/KMS) × cloud/model neutrality × per-regime
  packs × sovereignty (customer-hosted, their KMS).
- **ICP / wedge:** **India mid-market regulated FS first** — NBFCs, digital lenders, fintechs, insurers
  with acute RBI/DPDP pain but no platform team to DIY (Tier-1 build → chase later via SIs). Buyer =
  CRO/CCO; gatekeeper = CISO; champion = AI/platform engineer. **EU = the 2027 expansion.**

## 6. The 2026 plan
**Theme: one inspected reference customer; close the assurance gap; sharpen to "action firewall."**
1. **Close the assurance gap (highest priority — the #1 deal-blocker, not features):** commission the
   K·02 crypto review; **start SOC 2** (Type I→II); **book the pen-test**; counsel-sign the **DPA/MSA**
   (Waves 2–3). No regulated buyer closes without these.
2. **Land 1–2 design partners** (W7-1) in Indian regulated FS on an **internal, HITL-gated** agent use
   case (e.g. underwriting draft → human approve → sealed) — de-risks the "agents not in prod yet" TAM
   concern, shows gate+seal ROI fast.
3. **Frictionless integration:** drop-in SDK + the governed gateway (built) + a **governance MCP server**
   so any agent framework gets fail-closed governance in one integration. Ship a 10-minute "governed →
   sealed → here's the regulator-verifiable proof" demo as the activation moment.
4. **Reposition + category content** ("AI Action Governance"); lead with the proof bundle + neutrality.
5. **Start the SI channel** (EY/PwC/Deloitte/KPMG): free **Auditor View**; they mandate QUAICU when they
   audit a bank's AI → we become compliance infrastructure.
6. **Funding:** seed/Series-A on the "agentic security / Vault-for-AI-actions" narrative (infra →
   stickiness); enterprise-infra/security + India (Peak XV/Lightspeed/Cyberstarts) theses; proof points =
   design-partner LOIs + SOC 2 in progress.

## 7. The 2027 plan
**Theme: turn the reference into a repeatable motion; ride the EU Dec-2027 cliff.**
- Convert design partners → paying refs with **published audit/attestation**; productize the **RBI/DPDP
  compliance pack** as a premium SKU.
- **EU expansion** timed to **Dec 2027** (EU-region deploy/residency, AI-Act evidence pack, EU SIs).
- Scale the **SI channel**; add **AWS/Azure marketplace** listings (W7-3, build when pulled).
- Deepen the moat: SCIM/SSO (done), provable-erasure + KMS signers (done), **MCP governance** GA.
- Target: a handful of **$75k–$150k+ ACV** logos; an auditor/regulator recognizing the proof format.

## 8. Product focus — build vs. DON'T build
- **Build:** frictionless SDKs + framework adapters (LangChain/MCP) · fast HITL (Slack/Teams approve) ·
  the KMS-Merkle ledger + offline verifier (the moat) · turnkey per-regime policy packs · the governed
  gateway + MCP server · the dedicated/customer-KMS (RBI-grade) deployment.
- **DON'T build (partner/OSS):** prompt-injection (Lakera/Protect AI) · drift/quality (Fiddler/Arize) ·
  model-card/training docs (Credo AI). **Stay on the action lifecycle.** (Argues against over-investing
  in cloud/provider breadth before a design partner pulls it.)

## 9. Pricing & model
- Platform fee (SaaS or dedicated) **+ premium per-regime policy packs**; value-metric = **per governed
  action / ledger entry** (not per token). **ACV $75k–$150k+.** Low self-serve "land" tier for developer
  adoption; enterprise "expand" priced as **regulatory insurance**. (Enterprise is **consultation/MSA-led**
  — auto-subscription billing is deliberately out of scope; see W6-8 deferral.)

## 10. Top risks → mitigations
1. **Cooperative-enforcement perception ("advisory theater").** → Lead with **interception** (gateway,
   MCP proxy, zero-egress) so it's un-bypassable.
2. **Developer friction / latency.** → Drop-in middleware, low-latency gateway; never an app rewrite.
3. **Hyperscaler encroachment.** → Neutrality + customer-held-KMS sovereignty.
4. **TAM timing + EU cliff slipped to 2027.** → India-first (RBI now); internal draft-for-approval use
   cases for immediate ROI; EU as the 2027 wave.
5. **Assurance gap (SOC 2/pen-test/DPA missing).** → Gates every deal; **front-load Waves 2–3 over new
   features** — the single most important near-term action.

## 11. The honest bottom line
The **engineering is ahead of the market validation.** The scarce resource is not another adapter — it
is a **paying, inspected reference customer** and the **certifications** a regulated buyer's security
review demands. The 2026 plan is therefore mostly **non-engineering**: certs (Waves 2–3), a design
partner (W7-1), category positioning, and the SI channel — with engineering reserved for frictionless
integration (SDK/gateway/MCP) and whatever the design partner actually pulls.
