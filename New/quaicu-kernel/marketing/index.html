The governance kernel for AI.

QUAICU sits between your AI and the action it wants to take. Every model call or automated decision is checked against your policy before it runs, escalated to a human when it must be, and sealed into a tamper-evident ledger — so you can deploy AI in regulated environments and prove exactly what happened.

What it does

K·01

Policy enforcement

Author rules in CEL or import a regulatory pack (EU AI Act, India DPDP). Every action is allowed, denied, or routed for approval — fail-closed.

K·02

Tamper-evident ledger

Each decision is sealed into an RFC-6962 Merkle ledger, signed by a managed KMS key. Prove what your AI did, and that the record wasn't altered.

K·05

Governed AI gateway

Point your OpenAI-compatible SDK at the gateway. Bring your own model key; every call is governed and logged, then forwarded to your provider.

How it works

1

Connect

Sign up, get an API key, and import a policy pack — or write your own CEL rule in minutes.

2

Govern

Call /v1/authorize before you act, or route model calls through the gateway. QUAICU returns allow / deny / review.

3

Prove

Every decision is sealed to your ledger. Export the audit trail; show regulators the rule that governed each action.

curl -X POST https://kernel.quaicu.org/v1/authorize \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer qk_your_key" -H "X-Tenant-Id: your-tenant" \
  -d '{"type":"payments.wire","payload":{"amount":2500000}}'
# -> {"decision":"require_approval", "sealed": true, "ledger_seq": 42}
